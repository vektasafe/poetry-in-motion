# Poetry in Motion: Creator Agreement Template

**Parties:** Poetry in Motion (Vektasafe) and [Creator Name].

**Revenue share:** 40–50% of GMV (exact percentage confirmed at onboarding).  
**IP ownership:** Creator retains design ownership. Poetry in Motion receives a non‑exclusive license to display, market, and sell the designs on the platform.  
**Photo rights:** Creator grants Poetry in Motion the right to use product photos for platform display, marketing, and social media. Creator may use photos for their own promotion.  
**Payout timing:** Monthly, within 15 business days after the end of each calendar month.  
**Dispute resolution:** Any dispute shall first be resolved through mediation. If mediation fails, the dispute shall be resolved by arbitration in Nairobi, Kenya, under the rules of the Nairobi Centre for International Arbitration.  
**Term:** 12 months, renewable by mutual agreement. Either party may terminate with 30 days’ notice.

This template must be signed by all onboarded creators before their products are listed (MVP Honesty Requirement 7).
